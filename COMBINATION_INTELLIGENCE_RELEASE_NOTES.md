# Combination Intelligence v1.0

Shadow-mode analytics for historical combinations of signal features.

- Evaluates 2-factor and 3-factor combinations.
- Uses 24h AI Memory outcomes and normalized returns.
- Applies sample-size shrinkage so tiny segments do not dominate ranking.
- Shows best and weakest combinations relative to the global baseline.
- Includes Score, AI Quality, AI Risk, ML, liquidity, entry-price bucket,
  volume/liquidity changes, Similarity, category and direction.
- Does not block, promote, re-score or otherwise change live alerts.
- Telegram: `🧩 Combinations` button and `/combinations` command.
