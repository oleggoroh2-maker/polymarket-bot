# AI Calibration Engine v1.0

## Added
- Historical calibration using completed 24-hour AI Memory outcomes.
- Quality tiers: WATCH, GOOD and PREMIUM.
- Per-subscriber alert filters: all, good+premium, premium only.
- Calibration report in Telegram.
- Unified compact formatting for STRONG and AI Opportunity alerts.

## Important Telegram limitation
Telegram plain-text messages do not support changing font size inside a message. The analytical half is therefore made visually smaller through compact labels and shorter lines, not through an actual font-size change.
