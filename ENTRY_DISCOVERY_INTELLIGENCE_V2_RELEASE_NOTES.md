# Entry Discovery Intelligence v2

Shadow-only analytics layered on the unchanged Entry Discovery v1 generator.

- Segments EARLY outcomes by side, Category v2, entry-price bucket, Early Score and reasons.
- Adds selected two-feature combinations.
- Chronological 60/20/20 Discovery / Validation / Holdout report.
- Adds a pre-registered EARLY v2 score and future-only frozen challenger (score >=70).
- Future-only cutoff is initialized before new v1 candidates are inserted on a scan.
- No changes to live routing, Quality, Trade v2/v3, or Entry Discovery v1 thresholds.
