# Similarity Engine v2.1

- Adds weighted historical analog search for PUMP/DIP signals.
- Uses real 24-hour outcomes from AI Memory.
- Compares direction, price move, volume/liquidity changes, Score, AI Quality,
  AI Risk, ML, category, timeframe, liquidity and days left.
- Shows sample count, average similarity, strong/any continuation rates,
  average directional result and the best historical analog.
- Observation mode only: does not block alerts or change calibration scores.
