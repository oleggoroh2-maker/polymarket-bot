# AI Simulator v1.0 — Shadow Mode

- Stores a current-weight score and an Adaptive AI shadow-weight score for each new alert.
- After the 24-hour outcome, compares equal-sized top-ranked groups.
- Does not change live alerts, filters, quality tiers, or current weights.
- Telegram: button `🧪 AI Simulator` and command `/simulator`.
- First evaluated results appear about 24 hours after deployment.
