# Opportunity Engine v1

- Separate AI Opportunity notifications alongside event alerts.
- Criteria: Score, AI Quality, AI Risk, liquidity, price and market confirmation.
- 72-hour cooldown per market.
- Maximum 3 opportunity notifications per automatic scan.
- Markets already sent as STRONG PUMP/DIP in the same cycle are not duplicated.
- Opportunity alerts are recorded in the AI training database as `AI_OPPORTUNITY`.
